Team 14: Visualzing the Haunted Places Dataset

Overview: In this site, we want show some of the visuals one can make with d3 and our haunted places dataset. As outlined in our project report, we've taken many steps to enhance, featurize, and build upon locations of haunted sightings.

Data Sources: Data was collected from OpenStreetMap, the US Government, and other public sources. Contact afratto@usc.edu for more details.

Distribution of Sightings across the 50 States + D.C.

Our first visual is a simple pie chart, showing the frequency of sightings across the US. This was made possible by our use of a geoparser to extract locations out of our sightings.

Map Visualization

The map below shows each haunted sighting, with larger points representing clusters of haunted sightings, and the color showing proximity to places of worship. We can see a correlational trend, where clusters of haunted sightings are close to places of worship—Southern California being a big example.

Word Cloud Visualization

Using our descriptions of the haunted sightings, we created a word cloud to understand which words pop up the most frequently. Clearly, 'people', 'seen', 'night', and 'old' are recurring themes. The interactive visualization also lets viewers hover over words to see their exact occurrence counts, ensuring clarity and readability.

Moon Visualization

Our team added three new features to the haunted dataset, all pertaining to the moon the night of a haunting. The distance from the moon, the diameter of the moon, and the phase of the moon are all key parts of our story. From the graphs below, we can see there's a strong connection between each variable and the frequency of sightings.

Scatterplots of Witness Count, Binge Drinking, Semantic Similarity, and Fuzzy Similarity

Our dataset included a few important numerical values: Binge Drinking Rate, Witness Count, and our Fuzzy and Semantic Similarity scores—numeric indicators derived from the descriptions that allowed us to compare the text to other numeric data types. This allows us to see how binge drinking rate and witness count influence the descriptions.

Conclusion: This of course is just an exploratory analysis, as the visuals here merely demonstrate a correlation. Further work needs to be done to find causal effects. However, from this project it's clear that religiosity, moon phases, and geography seem to play roles in the occurrence of haunted sightings.
