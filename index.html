Team 14: Visualizing the Haunted Places Dataset

Overview: In this site, we show some of the visuals one can make with D3 and our haunted places dataset. As outlined in our project report, we've taken many steps to enhance, featurize, and build upon locations of haunted sightings.

Data Sources: Data was collected from OpenStreetMap, the US Government, and other public sources. Contact afratto@usc.edu for more details.

Distribution of Sightings across the 50 States + D.C.

A simple pie chart shows the frequency of sightings across the US. This was made possible by our use of a geoparser to extract locations from our sightings.

Map Visualization

This map shows each haunted sighting. Larger points = clusters; color = proximity to places of worship. Southern California is a major hotbed!

Word Cloud Visualization

Using haunting descriptions, we built a word cloud to highlight common language. 'People', 'seen', and 'night' are frequently repeated — very spooky!

Moon Visualization

We added three new moon-related features: distance, diameter, and phase on the night of the haunting. Some eerie patterns emerge...

Scatterplots: Witness Count, Binge Drinking, Similarities

These scatterplots help us compare textual similarity with numeric values like binge drinking and witness count. Booze, crowds, and ghosts... what's the link?

Conclusion: These visuals show fascinating trends. While not causation, there's clear correlation between sightings, geography, religion, and even the moon.
