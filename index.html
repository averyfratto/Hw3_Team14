Team 14: Visualizing the Haunted Places Dataset

Pie Chart Map Word Cloud Moon Scatter

Overview: This site presents visuals built with D3.js using our haunted places dataset. As outlined in our project report, we enhanced, featurized, and explored patterns within locations of paranormal sightings.

Data Sources: Data was collected from OpenStreetMap, the US Government, and other public sources. Contact afratto@usc.edu for more details.

Distribution of Sightings across the 50 States + D.C.

A pie chart showing frequency of sightings across states. Made using a geoparser to extract and count locations.

Map Visualization

Each haunted sighting shown on the map; larger points = clusters; colors = proximity to religious places. Notable patterns emerge in Southern California.

Word Cloud Visualization

A word cloud from haunting descriptions. Hover to see frequency. Common themes: 'people', 'seen', 'night', 'old'.

Moon Visualization

We added moon features: distance, diameter, and phase. These show strong patterns related to sighting frequency.

Scatterplots of Witness Count, Binge Drinking, and Text Similarities

Numerical insights from witness count, binge drinking, fuzzy/semantic similarity. How do external data impact descriptions?

Conclusion: This is exploratory analysis. While correlations emerge (religiosity, moon phases, geography), further study is needed to determine causality.
